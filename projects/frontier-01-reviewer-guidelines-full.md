## NeurIPS 2025 Reviewer Guidelines - Full Text
Source: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/ReviewerGuidelines (4 chunks fetched)

Thank you for agreeing to serve as a reviewer for NeurIPS 2025!

Contact: Area Chair assigned should be first point of contact via OpenReview comment with AC as reader. If unable resolve with AC contact program chairs via https://neurips.cc/Help/Contact?select=Program.

Important Dates: Bids May 17-21, Check Assignments May 29, Reviewing May 29 - Jul 2 (late/low quality could lose access to reviews on own co-authored papers), Author Rebuttal Jul 24-30, Reviewer-Author Discussions Jul 31-Aug 6, Reviewer-AC Discussions Aug 7-13, Author Notification Sept 18.

Main Tasks:
1. Preparation: Read code of conduct, responsible reviewing blogpost https://blog.neurips.cc/2025/05/02/responsible-reviewing-initiative-for-neurips-2025/, OpenReview profile up to date, check conflicts, assignments may change first week.
2. Bid on papers May 17-21 important input, deceptive bidding ethics investigation malicious actors removed.
3. Check assignments May 29 log in OpenReview check conflicts expertise, if not qualified communicate AC right away.
4. Write thoughtful reviews May 29-Jul 2 time consuming but community depends high quality, informative substantiated, superficial uninformed worse than no review, if argue lack novelty provide references point to existing mechanisms, thoroughly comment technical aspects not only organisation grammar, see review form questions, flag questionable papers ethics review, use paper checklist included, answering no typically not grounds rejection, reward upfront limitations, minor format violations few lines ignore but major report AC, reviews and meta-reviews of accepted papers as well as discussion will be made public but reviewer SAC/AC identities anonymous, authors rejected option make public.
5. Read author responses and discuss Jul 31-Aug 13 carefully read other reviews and author responses, keep open mind even if response didn't change opinion acknowledge read and considered, rolling discussion with authors via comment visible to them, participating critical especially borderline differing assessments, if evaluation changed revise review explain change, consensus valuable only rarely unanimous overruled not mandatory.
6. After discussion ACs make initial accept/reject decisions with SACs before Sept 18, workload light but if ACs questions respond promptly.

Review Form:
01 Summary: Briefly summarize paper and contributions in own understanding after reading, not critique, not paste abstract, authors should generally agree well-written summary.
02 Strengths and Weaknesses: Thorough assessment reasons accept/reject touching:
- Quality: Technically sound? Claims well supported theoretical analysis experimental results? Methods appropriate? Complete piece or WIP? Careful honest evaluating strengths weaknesses?
- Clarity: Clearly written? Well organized? Adequately inform reader? Superbly written provides enough info for expert to reproduce.
- Significance: Impactful community? Others likely use ideas build on them? Difficult task better than previous? Advance understanding demonstrable? Unique data conclusions theoretical experimental approach?
- Originality: New insights deepen understanding highlight important properties existing methods? Clear how differs previous with citations? Novel tasks methods advance field? Novel combination reasoning well-articulated? Originality does NOT require entirely new method. Novel insights evaluating existing methods improved efficiency fairness equally valuable. Markdown LaTeX allowed.
03 Quality: 4 excellent 3 good 2 fair 1 poor
04 Clarity: 4 excellent 3 good 2 fair 1 poor
05 Significance: 4 excellent 3 good 2 fair 1 poor
06 Originality: 4 excellent 3 good 2 fair 1 poor
07 Questions: List 3-5 actionable with clear criteria under which evaluation score could increase/decrease very important for rebuttal discussion.
08 Limitations: Adequately addressed limitations and potential negative societal impact? If yes leave yes else constructive suggestions, reward rather than punish being upfront, think critical points missing feedback.
09 Overall: 6 Strong Accept Technically flawless groundbreaking impact one or more areas AI exceptionally strong evaluation reproducibility resources no unaddressed ethics, 5 Accept Technically solid high impact at least one sub-area or moderate-high more than one good-to-excellent evaluation resources reproducibility no unaddressed ethics, 4 Borderline accept solid reasons accept outweigh reject limited evaluation sparingly, 3 Borderline reject solid reasons reject outweigh accept, 2 Reject technical flaws weak eval inadequate reproducibility incompletely addressed ethics, 1 Strong Reject well-known results unaddressed ethics
10 Confidence: 5 absolutely certain familiar checked math details carefully, 4 confident unlikely but not impossible not understand some parts unfamiliar some related work, 3 fairly confident possible not understand some parts unfamiliar some pieces Math/other details not carefully checked, 2 willing defend but quite likely not understand central parts unfamiliar some pieces not carefully checked, 1 educated guess not in area difficult understand not carefully checked.
11 Ethical concerns flag ethics review guidance https://neurips.cc/public/EthicsGuidelines
12 Code of conduct acknowledgement abide NeurIPS code of conduct
13 Responsible reviewing acknowledgement read responsible reviewing initiatives https://blog.neurips.cc/2025/05/02/responsible-reviewing-initiative-for-neurips-2025/

Other Roles: ACs principal contact ensuring quality reviews facilitating discussions writing meta-reviews evaluating quality reviews making decision recommendations, SACs oversee small number ACs helping find expert reviewers calibrating decisions discussing borderline helping PCs final decisions, Ethics Reviewers flagged submissions additional review comments visible all reviewers AC authors.

Best Practices: Be thoughtful first year graduate student, Be fair no personal feelings, Be useful constructive, Be specific no vague, Be flexible update understanding when new info, Be timely respect deadlines respond promptly discussion, avoid discriminatory criteria rude offensive, if pressures notify program chairs, if unethical suspect notify AC.

FAQ: similar work workshop allowed don't mention, suspect plagiarism reach AC, emergency deadline reach AC, can accept/reject all based merits not match acceptance rate, not required read supplementary but welcome, never break anonymity independent review even if aware, if already reviewed at other venue don't assume unchanged read carefully high quality, sub-reviewers not allowed conflicts cannot checked.

Policies: Call For Papers https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers, Paper checklist https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist, Ethics review https://neurips.cc/public/EthicsGuidelines, Executing code untrusted vulnerabilities Docker container VM network-isolated cloud, Confidentiality keep everything confidential not use ideas code results until publicly available not talk share without approval, Code submitted cannot distributed used other purpose, Double-blind reviewing double blind reviewers ACs cannot see author identities but not SACs PCs authors responsible anonymizing submissions including supplementary linked material code, if not adequately anonymized contact AC don't attempt find identities searching arXiv active violation, Formatting 9 content pages including figures tables submission style additional pages only references checklist allowed additional content reviewed at discretion lenient minor spillover page 10 easily rectified final version flag violations not easily rectified, Dual submissions not allowed identical substantially similar papers in submission accepted published other archival venues identical substantially similar other NeurIPS submissions distinct substantial slicing thinly discouraged may fall under dual submission policy suspect contact AC, Useful Links Call For Papers, checklist, ethics review.
